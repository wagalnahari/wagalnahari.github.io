Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

App Name: n8n.TikTok

n8n.TikTok Privacy Commitment: We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our video processing service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 TikTok API Data (via OAuth):

  • Basic profile information (with your consent)
  • Video upload and publishing permissions
  • TikTok user ID for service functionality

1.2 Video Content:

  • Videos you upload for processing
  • Processed video output
  • Associated metadata (duration, format, size)

1.3 Technical Information:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Usage statistics and error logs
  • API request timestamps

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process and optimize your videos using FFmpeg
  • Upload processed videos to your TikTok account (with your explicit permission)
  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Monitor service performance and security
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Retention

3.1 Video Storage:

  • Uploaded videos: Encrypted and stored temporarily for processing
  • Processed videos: Available for 24 hours, then automatically deleted
  • No permanent video storage on our servers

3.2 API Tokens:

  • TikTok OAuth tokens are securely stored for session management
  • Tokens are encrypted and can be revoked by users at any time

4. TikTok API Integration

n8n.TikTok uses TikTok for Developers API with the following scopes:

  • video.upload: To upload processed videos as drafts
  • video.publish: To post videos to your TikTok feed (with user confirmation)
  • user.info.basic: To display your connected account

All TikTok data usage complies with TikTok's Platform Policies.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption for video transfers
  • Secure OAuth token storage
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only:

  • With TikTok as necessary for API functionality
  • To comply with legal requirements
  • To protect our rights and safety

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke TikTok API permissions at any time
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.
